Email support

For bugs, questions, corrections or feature requests, send an email. Include your app version (Settings → About → Version) so I can reproduce the issue.

azbarks@gmail.com

For bug reports, include:

  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Steps to reproduce it (the simpler the better)
  • Your iOS version and Spanish Helper version (Settings → About)
  • The word or phrase involved, if there was one

I typically respond within a few days. ¡Gracias por tu paciencia!
