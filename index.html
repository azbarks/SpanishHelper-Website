What's inside

Everyday Spanish, one word at a time

📚

The words that matter most

Nearly 1,000 of the most common words in Mexican Spanish plus everyday phrases, ready in your Word Bank — most common first. Add your own any time.

🔁

Comes back until it sticks

Tap ✓ or ✗. Words you know come up less often; words you miss come back sooner. Nothing piles up if you skip a day.

↔️

Two steps to really know it

First you see the Spanish and recognize it. Then you see the English and say it in Spanish. Only then is it Learned.

💬

Phrases, not just words

Every common word comes with real example sentences. One tap turns a sentence into a phrase you study — and the next example appears.

🎧

Listen in the car

Hands-free: it says the English, pauses for you to answer out loud, then says the Spanish. Works with the screen locked and your steering-wheel buttons.

📖

Stories and conversations

57 short, easy stories that show how much you already understand, little dialogues for real situations, and verb tables for 236 verbs.

How it works

Ten minutes on the Today tab

1

Start a few words

Tap Learn Something New to start the next most common words — or sort the Word Bank and mark the ones you already know.

2

Tap, then ✓ or ✗

Tap a card to see the answer and hear it. Be honest — the app uses your answers to decide when to show it again.

3

Watch it become Learned

After a few right days in a row, the app asks if you're ready for the next step — and later, whether you've learned it.

No account. No internet. No streak guilt.

Spanish Helper works on a plane or in the car, and nothing piles up if you take a day off — tomorrow just picks up where you left off.
