Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026  ·  App version 1.0

Spanish My Way collects no personal data and sends nothing over the internet.

Your words and progress stay on your device. We have no servers, no analytics, and no way to see what you study. The app asks for no permissions — no microphone, no camera, no location, no contacts, no notifications.

What data does the app store?

Spanish My Way stores only what it needs to teach you:

  • The built-in words and phrases, with your progress on each — whether you're studying it, its schedule, right and missed answers, and when you last saw it
  • Any words or phrases you add or edit yourself
  • Your settings, and which stories you've finished

This data is saved in the app's own storage on your device. It is not synced to iCloud. It is included in your device's regular iCloud or computer backup, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Does the app connect to the internet?

No. Spanish My Way makes no network requests of its own, and it works the same in airplane mode. The Spanish voice is Apple's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on your iPhone.

Links you choose to tap open outside the app: the Watch link on the Talk tab opens a YouTube channel in the YouTube app or Safari (once you leave Spanish My Way, YouTube's own privacy policy applies), and the Help, Website, Support and Privacy links in Settings open this website in Safari.

What about backups I export?

Settings → Backup → Export Backup creates a file of your words and progress and opens the share sheet. You decide where it goes — Files, AirDrop, email. Spanish My Way doesn't send it anywhere on its own.

Does the app collect analytics or crash reports?

No. Spanish My Way contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, and no telemetry of any kind. We receive no data about how you use the app.

Does the app use advertising or tracking?

No. There are no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Children's privacy

Spanish My Way does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us and we'll respond promptly.
